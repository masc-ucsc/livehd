2r-route Legroute as the final routability step of placement

Plan written 2026-11-15 from a question-and-answer session, after the URoute calibration (3u-uroute) and the SAT legroute work (2l-legroute) both failed their goals. This page replaces those two pages; their history is in git. Nothing on this page is implemented yet. Names of options, targets and directories are proposals.

Remove the learned congestion model (URoute) and the SAT-based legroute stages, and rebuild legroute as one step at the end of placement: a FastRoute-style global router and a per-grid-cell legality check alternate until the placement is as dense as it can be while still routable. The router proves that routes fit the track capacity of every grid cell. The legality check proves that the standard cells the router assumed inside a grid cell can really be placed there, which a global router never checks. The name legroute stays: it is still route-aware legalization.

Why

  • URoute did not earn its place. Trained on 121 routed HighTide layouts (10 designs, asap7 / nangate45 / sky130hd, macros, high-density and aspect-ratio stress cases), the best recipe reached parity with RSMT on map correlation for unseen designs (Pearson 0.662 vs 0.659) and stayed below it on Spearman, SSIM and hotspot overlap. RSMT ordered placements of the same design by routed congestion far better (pairwise concordance 0.90–0.98 vs 0.4–0.7). In the placement loop URoute left lfsr, minimax and liteeth bit-identical to RSMT while costing 6–59× the wall time (about 15 s per call on x86).
  • RSMT cannot trigger. The same study measured RSMT's utilization at about 0.33 of the routed value, so its util > 1 overflow test almost never fires. Placement needs a signal with a real capacity scale. A global router on the track grid has one.
  • SAT legroute did not close. Local and Final never produced complete native routing on SHA3, minimax or ternip (2l-legroute). The code is about 20k lines plus about 11k lines of tests, and Plan carries more machinery (cell boxes, partitions, admission) that only exists to feed it.
  • LiveHD placements are hard to route. With OpenROAD routing LiveHD's placement, nangate45 minimax had 112K detailed-route violations at iteration 3, nangate45 liteeth 38K, and sky130hd liteeth failed global routing on congestion. These are the cases the new step must fix.
  • Most of the router exists. Legroute Plan already routes the converged placement with a negotiated A* router with rip-up, has an opt-in port of FastRoute's L/Z patterns (fast_route_pattern.cpp), a window fit check (assess_window_fit) and can feed its congestion back into DREAMPlace (plan.route_model=coarse).

The step

Input: the converged global placement (not legal), the fixed die, core, rows, macros and power grid, and the routing tracks. Output: a legal placement and a global route that fits it, or a report of the grid cells that could not be closed.

  1. Grid. Build the routing grid with a grid cell of N tracks per side. Edge capacity comes from the real tracks minus power, macros, blockages and the clock charge. Cell capacity is the free row sites inside the grid cell.
  2. Assign. Every movable cell belongs to the grid cell that holds it. A grid cell over its site capacity sends its excess to nearby grid cells that have both site room and routing slack (minimum displacement, timing-critical cells last).
  3. Route. Route all nets on the grid: pattern seeds where enabled, then negotiated rip-up and reroute. Later rounds reroute only the nets whose pins moved.
  4. Prove and freeze. A grid cell whose cells fit and whose edges have slack is legalized with greedy plus Abacus restricted to that grid cell. If the legalizer succeeds, the cells are frozen there and the routes through it are kept.
  5. Repair. For grid cells that fail (sites or routing), free only the cells in and around them, inflate where routing is short, and resume global placement with everything else fixed. Then go back to step 2 for the freed cells.
  6. Finish. Stop when every grid cell is frozen or the round budget is spent. Legalize what is left with the regular legalizer, route once on the legal pins and report overflow, unfrozen grid cells, displacement, HPWL and timing.

Decisions that stay open inside the step, to be settled by measurement: the grid-cell size, how much routing slack a grid cell needs before it is frozen, how far the repair region extends around a failing grid cell, and whether a frozen grid cell may be reopened when a neighbour cannot close.

One seed is not enough for a small difference: minimax at utilization 60 moved HPWL between 111k and 129k µm from a density change of 0.0002, so close results need several runs.

What is kept from the URoute work

  • The routing recipes (prebuilt OpenROAD plus ORFS scripts, LiveHD placement handoff) and the 121-layout corpus, under /lscratch/renau/uroute_train.
  • The scorer (R2), so the router's congestion map can be compared with routed utilization when a result needs explaining.
  • Three tool defects found on the way, to fix or drop with the scorer: 4 of 7 nangate45 liteeth layouts fail the Python-versus-native feature check, the label extractor rejects non-preferred wires on sky130's single top layer, and the DEF reader rejects the non-default routing rules that OpenROAD CTS writes.

Direction after this

Once the step is on by default, the next goal is PCRS: native distribution of power, clock, reset and scan chains, as an alternative to OpenROAD CTS and a stepping stone to native routing in LiveHD. The SAT attempt at native routing failed, so that needs a new plan. It is recorded here only to keep the direction clear; it is not part of this task.
