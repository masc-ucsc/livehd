domino Domino-logic synthesis (pass.domino)

Map the register-to-register combinational cones that fit into domino gates: unate series-parallel NMOS networks with fan-in k = 8, series stack D = 4, at most B = 16 transistors, two gate levels per cycle (one per clock phase), dual rail built only where a reader needs the complement. Cones that do not fit stay static and go to pass.abc. The design point, the algorithm and the acceptance numbers come from the Domino Design Space study (2026-09-20): 35.9 % of outputs pooled over 48 circuits, 51.7 % on EPFL control, 4 to 6 % of the logic. Since 2026-09-24 the cover itself is upstream's pass/usyn; this task adds the minimum-transistor gate model, the dual-rail-on-demand model and the domino backend. Full design and primer: pass/domino/README.md.

Design notes

  • A domino gate is non-inverting: the cut function must be positive unate in the rails it reads. Negative rails are free at flops (Q̄) and cost a twin gate inside a cone. The twin implements the dual function over the opposite rails, so its stack is the widest prime implicate: an OR8 fits at stack 1, its twin is an 8-deep stack.
  • Minimum-transistor factoring is branch-and-bound on the transistor count. Always pass the gate budget: unbounded, the symmetric 8-variable functions take minutes; with B = 16 they are decided in under 40 ms.
  • Domino gates are modelled as combinational for LEC and simulation; φ1/φ2 are structural only. The LEC verdict is not a timing proof.
