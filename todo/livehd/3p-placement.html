Depends on

No blocking prerequisite tasks. Backend extensions must use the native C++ DREAMPlace boundary, the flat placed graph and in-memory OpenTimer RC/STA.

Subtasks — pending

  • E — Independent validation and larger-design measurements.
    • Add an optional OpenROAD check_placement leg and compare with stock DREAMPlace on identical netlists, LEF/DEF, constraints and resource budgets. A requested external leg must fail if its tool is missing. Establish measured HPWL, timing and runtime differences before choosing a parity threshold.
    • Measure full SKY130 and ASAP7 designs, including high fanout, multiple memories, tight whitespace and incremental edits. Declare benchmark inputs and technology dependencies in the benchmark repository; keep default LiveHD tests self-contained.
    • Report legality separately from GP overflow/convergence. Sweep bins and iteration budgets: a small trial legalized at 32 bins/500 iterations without reaching the GP target, while 16/1500 reached it. Do not generalize that tuning or change defaults from one fixture.
    • Attribute gains with separate timing-seed, placed-RC refinement, sizing, Steiner repair and cone ablations. Small SKY130 trials do not establish large-design QoR; the ALU rejected later weighting and repair trials while the memory accepted a weighting refinement. Retain rejection reasons, residual violations, area and displacement alongside delay.
    • Run synthesis trials serially with process-tree memory and wall-time limits. Validate emitted netlists independently. Measure cold and warm incremental runs with a frozen binary; after a rebuild, run the warm command twice before judging reuse.
  • C — Improve bounded Steiner repair quality and coverage.
    • Improve buffer selection and branch/repeater placement using actual driver strength, sink loads and available Liberty limits. Compare alternative topologies and drive ladders before considering optimal Steiner dynamic programming. The constructive heuristic can insert many buffers without improving the objective.
    • Preflight row capacity and local whitespace for repair candidates. A larger buffer trial failed legalization in the fixed frame; prefer feasible alternatives and explicit refusals over silently growing an imported floorplan or moving FIXED objects.
    • Address repair's 1000-distinct-location FLUTE limit and 256-new-buffer candidate limit with bounded partitioning or staged repair. Simply raising limits is not a scalability solution. Preserve shared trunks, packed-bit connectivity, protected clock/reset networks and fixed-cell behavior.
    • Improve residual-violation reporting and candidate prioritization under the 24-trial/eight-accept budget per sizing or repair phase. Distinguish exhausted search, missing characterization, unsupported geometry and timing rejection. Missing Liberty limits must not be treated as verified electrical closure.
    • Keep whole-core legality and fresh RC/STA as acceptance gates. Preserve the existing delay, setup, hold-violation and electrical no-regression checks; a lower planning cost alone cannot justify retaining a repair.
  • M1 — Broader physical inputs and more accurate RC.
    • Support row gaps, placement blockages and additional row orientations/layouts. The current contiguous equal-width N/FS-row boundary needs explicit compatibility and legality checks before accepting broader DEF inputs.
    • Add opt-in characterized hard-memory LEF/Liberty binding and broader mixed-size placement. Keep estimated square proxies and clock-boundary zero-internal-delay timing explicit when characterization is unavailable; do not report proxy timing as characterized memory timing.
    • Replace the high-fanout Manhattan-star RC fallback beyond FLUTE's 1000-location limit with a bounded shared-trunk estimate, coordinated with C's fanout splitting. Check that changes improve RC accuracy rather than merely lowering reported delay.
    • Compare placement RC with routed reference parasitics, then evaluate routing layers, vias, coupling and clock-tree effects according to measured error. Add optional diagnostic SPEF export if needed for this comparison while keeping the optimization loop in memory.
  • M2 — Timing optimizer refinements.
    • Add timing/overflow checkpoints inside a solver call and evaluate retaining Nesterov state between weight updates. The current post-synthesis seed and placed-RC rounds restart the solver; compare total work and final QoR before replacing that baseline.
    • Evaluate slacks after virtual buffering, following the findResizeSlacks approach. Journal and restore temporary edits so weight estimation cannot leak unaccepted buffers or timing state into the graph.
    • Evaluate adaptive weight selection, smoothing and early stopping using the ablations in E. A rejected round is useful evidence; do not force acceptance or increase weights merely to make every round change placement.
    • Evaluate cheaper GP-only exploration followed by full legalization and fresh RC/STA before retaining any candidate. Approximate overlapping placement cannot establish the acceptance timing result.
  • D — Incremental update and displacement refinements.
    • Add insert/delete/rewire observer hooks and affected-net RC/STA invalidation to avoid rebuilding every private trial. Keep graph, physical pin identities, geometry and timing synchronized; preserve rejection rollback.
    • Evaluate an explicit per-cell displacement radius in addition to expanding ECO windows. Single-cell whitespace search keeps unchanged neighbors fixed, but its search covers the core; a selected window alone is not a per-cell bound.
    • Evaluate compatible optimizer-state reuse, including its storage and invalidation cost. Check topology, masters, rows, IO, fixed objects and solver configuration before restoring state. Extend tests to multi-block edits and cones crossing block boundaries while retaining stable block/color provenance.
  • M3 — Broader proved critical-cone resynthesis.
    • Support negative cut-input arrivals with consistent time origins and backend constraints; do not clamp them to zero. Validate required times, slew/load units and the resulting placed timing independently.
    • Evaluate larger cuts and cuts through wiring aliases. The current 32-cell/depth-16, 12-input/eight-output bounds make exhaustive proof tractable; larger support needs a bounded independent proof strategy before raising those limits. Preserve every crossing side output and individual packed bits.
    • Add a native USYN or other provider through the ABC-free mapping seam. Preserve actual boundary arrival/slew/required/load constraints, exact function validation, seeded legal placement and fresh RC/STA acceptance. Keep state, memory, fixed and protected-network barriers until separately supported.
  • M4 — Macro placement and floorplanning.
    • Select a permissively licensed backend and verify the selected revision's license before integration. Evaluate the native DREAMPlace macro flow or an OpenROAD mpl adapter through the existing floorplan boundary.
    • Add the planned synth.place.macro= stage for hard macros and memories, producing FIXED macro positions for standard-cell placement. Account for halos, channels, pin access and whitespace needed by later repair.
    • Co-optimize macro positions and IO pins under timing feedback, with independent mixed-size legality checks. Compare against deterministic proxy packing before adding more floorplanning complexity.
