2is-incsynth Placement-aware incremental color resynthesis

Close the loop between placement and synthesis per color. Every placement stage saves the timing it measured at each color's IO pins (the equivalent of SDC set_input_delay/set_output_delay for the color) and a per-color wire-load table derived from the placed RC. A color whose critical path moves because of those IO delays is resynthesized as a whole, starting from its already mapped netlist, with the delays and the wire load applied. The first synthesis of a run uses its normal configured flow with zero IO delays; the loop closes once, automatically, after the first placement stage of a run, and the user drives further iterations by re-running lhd synth on the same workdir.

Implementation and focused validation in progress The typed placement-stage feedback, scoped ABC wire loads, mapped-body remap, bounded CEC, cache update and single-restart orchestration are implemented. End-to-end QoR evaluation and architecture reselection (I9) remain pending. Re-evaluate the placement feedback with the corrected interconnect timing model; the hierarchy-FP case must also validate centroid feasibility after cell growth from resizing/resynthesis. Gate-level color remapping does not yet reconsider the semantic arithmetic/shift implementation alternatives of I9. Persistent placement feedback is bound to the timing-engine source identity; a timer change clears old measurements before the pre-placement trigger while retaining the mapping's used constraints and geometry-derived wire tables. ABC is the only mapper in scope; usyn is a follow-on (I8).

Depends on

Nothing outstanding must finish first. It builds on three landed pieces: 2b-boundary (the per-region SC_Bnd environment: per-PI driver/arrival, per-PO load/virtual delay; pass/abc/abc_boundary.hpp), the incremental region cache (pass/synth/region_cache.hpp, one row per region module <top>__c<color> under <workdir>/abc_cache) and the staged native placement of 3p-placement (placement::place() runs FP+macro as one place_single and the final cells as a second one). The placed-cone resynthesis (synth.place.resynth, resynthesize_cones) stays as is: it resynthesizes up to four small cones with local placement; this task resynthesizes whole colors between placement stages.

Why

Synthesis cuts the design into colors (pass/color) and maps each color as its own module. A color's ports are ABC primary inputs and outputs, and today they carry zero input and output delay: the partition may cut a path anywhere between two flops, yet every color is mapped as if the path started and ended at its ports. The built-in flow optimizes area (the delay budget only drives the sizing tail, and &nf -D is ignored by ABC), there is no realistic frequency target per color beyond period - reg_margin, and nothing from placement (wire RC, real arrivals) ever reaches the mapper. refine_boundaries (Phase B of 2b-boundary) already propagates arrival/required times across regions and re-sizes cells, but from the stitched netlist with zero wire load, and it swaps cells only; it never re-maps a color.

This is acceptable while synthesis and placement are independent. With placement-aware synthesis the color must be mapped against what the placer measured: its real IO arrivals/required times and the average interconnect its nets see.

Rulings (decided 2026-10-19)

  1. IO timing is, per color port bit, the placed-STA input arrival (color inputs) and output required (color outputs), in ps relative to the clock: the SDC set_input_delay/set_output_delay equivalents fed to the ABC (later usyn) delay feedback. Loads and slews are not part of this contract; interconnect goes through the wire-load table.
  2. Resynthesis starts from the color's mapped netlist, not from the RTL partition: the mapped body is read back into the mapper, the IO delays and the wire load are added, and the full flow runs again (the mapped structure is the starting point, the mapper may restructure it).
  3. Trigger = criticality, not delta. A color is resynthesized only when applying the newly measured IO delays to its last synthesized netlist makes a path critical (violating, or within the margin of the period) and the IO delay on that path changed by more than the option (default 5% of the period). Removing an input delay never triggers; adding an output delay that still leaves slack never triggers. A color that stays non-critical is never resynthesized, however much its IO numbers moved. Since the first synthesis assumes 0/0, the first placement will fire on essentially every color that is already near critical.
  4. Stage precedence and netlist identity. Within a run: final overrides macro overrides FP. Across runs, the stored IO delays are bound to the color's synthesized netlist: an incremental hit keeps the stored last-final numbers and FP does not retrigger on an unchanged design; a changed color (cache miss, resynthesized from RTL) has its IO delays deleted and is treated as first-seen, so the next FP/macro step will likely fire for it. The wire-load tables survive RTL changes: they are a property of the technology and the color's average connectivity, not of the netlist.
  5. Restart the first stage reached. After a resynthesis, placement restarts at the stage that ran first in this run: fp_plan when FP ran, the macro stage when there is no FP, or the final cell placement when the block has no macros. The remaining stages then follow.
  6. Once per run, then warnings. The automatic resynthesis fires at most once per run, right after the first placement stage. Later stages only save their timing and print a warning for colors that would fire. The next iteration is the user re-running lhd synth on the same workdir: synthesis is an incremental hit, the stored timing (last final) is compared with what each color was last synthesized with, and critical colors are resynthesized before placement starts (then rule 5 applies).
  7. One knob, workdir required. synth.place.io_resynth=<percent>, default 5, is both the critical-path threshold and the switch; 0 disables the trigger and the warning. All state lives under --workdir; without a workdir nothing is stored and nothing fires, and a nonzero explicit io_resynth without a workdir is a usage error.
  8. RC feedback = one Liberty wire_load model per color. ABC's SCL timer honors wire_load tables (fanout count to wire length, times a capacitance per length, with slope extrapolation) through the -c flag of buffer/upsize/dnsize/ stime (../abc/src/map/scl/sclLoad.c, Abc_SclFindWireLoadModel). One table per color, because colors differ in average connectivity even on the same technology. Capacitance only for now.
  9. ABC only. usyn consumes the same stored inputs later (I8).

Facts to confirm first (I0)

  • Flat pin to region port. Placement flattens the mapped netlist (partition::flatten_hierarchy, pass/place/place.cpp near the cannot flatten mapped netlist refusal) and the STA keys its per-pin report by flat pin name (Timing_feedback::pins, pass/place/repair.hpp). Mapped cells carry attrs::synth_region_id, which pass.opentimer already uses for its per-region Color_qor rows. What is missing is the map from a crossing net (driver region differs from a sink region) back to (region module, port name, bit). Confirm whether the content-stable port names of the region modules survive into the flat net names, or whether the Flat_origin_map argument of flatten_hierarchy (placement passes nullptr today) gives that map for free.
  • Mapped-body import. Phase B already re-imports every region's mapped body into an ABC netlist (abc_boundary.cpp, the Abc_NtkToLogic/Abc_NtkDupDfs path). Confirm that strash of that network plus the built-in flow and a region-writer emission into a scratch module, followed by copy_body_from into the region module (the cache's restore path), keeps port names and the LEC twin contract. Latches (plan.preserves_latches) and the QN encoding gate (builtin_flow_) must hold on the re-import exactly as on a cold map.
  • Where the mapper sees IO delays. &nf ignores -D and has no arrival input; the IO delays act through the SC_Bnd table on the sizing/buffering tail, on time_ntk (the budget judgement that drives the area_relax_pct re-map ladder) and on the exact re-size. Confirm the ladder reads the same timed slack the table changes, so a color that is now over budget because of its arrivals is re-mapped for delay instead of relaxed for area. Record the gap (structural mapping is arrival-blind) in I8.
  • Units. Stored times are ps; Boundary_table::set_pi/set_po take ps; the STA report carries time_unit_ps; the Liberty wire_load lengths are in the library's length unit and cap in its capacitance unit per length. Pin the conversions once (placement's Wire_rc is ohm/um and fF/um).
  • Stage names. The FP stage and the macro stage both live inside the first place_single (fp_place, macro_mode); the final cell placement is the second. Pin which stage is "first reached" for the four combinations (FP+macro+final, macro+final, final only, GP-only mode where the loop is off).
  • Per-lane libraries. Every parallel mapping lane has its own ABC frame and read_lib, so a per-color SC_WireLoad injected into the lane's SC_Lib cannot leak across colors mapped in parallel. Confirm, and confirm the injection is removed before the next region of the same lane.

Subtasks

  • I1 — Harvest per-color IO timing from each placement stage.
    • After every completed stage (FP, macro, final) run the placed STA the stage already runs (evaluate() in lhd_kernel_synth.cpp, or the FP checkpoint's final evaluation) and reduce Timing_feedback::pins to crossing nets: per region module, per input port bit the arrival at the sink pin inside the region, per output port bit the required time at the driver pin inside the region. Clock and reset nets are excluded (they are ideal in the mapper too). A primary input/output of the top keeps its SDC delay.
    • Store under <workdir>/abc_cache/io_timing.json: {schema, period_ps, regions: {module: {netlist: <cache digest>, used: {in: {port: ps}, out: {port: ps}}, placed: {stage, run_id, in: {...}, out: {...}}}}}. used is what the color's current mapped body was synthesized with (absent after a cold map = 0/0); placed is the latest measurement, replaced only by a stage of equal or higher rank in the same run or by any stage of a later run whose netlist digest still matches (ruling 4).
    • Bind to netlist identity through the region cache row: on a cache miss or a cold map the region's entry is deleted; on a hit it is kept. The wire-load entry (I4) is never deleted by a miss.
    • Emit a per-stage diagnostic row per color: ports, worst input arrival, worst output required, and how many bits moved past the threshold.
  • I2 — Trigger: criticality under the new IO delays.
    • For each color with a stored placed entry, re-time its mapped body with the SCL timer (Abc_SclManStart as in Phase B) under a Boundary_table filled from placed plus the color's wire load, against the region budget (region_budget). The result is the color's worst slack with the measured environment.
    • Fire when (a) that slack is negative or within io_resynth% of the period, and (b) on that critical path the IO delay changed by more than io_resynth% of the period versus used. A tightening with slack left, or a loosening, never fires. Removing a delay never fires (ruling 3).
    • The same evaluation produces the end-of-stage warning when the stage is not the first of the run (ruling 6): one line per color that would fire, with the path endpoints and the delta.

OptionDefaultMeaning
synth.place.io_resynth5Percent of the clock period. A color is resynthesized when its critical path under the placed IO delays is violating or within this margin and the IO delay on that path moved by more than this versus the last synthesis. 0 disables the trigger and the end-of-stage warning. Risks

  • Oscillation. A resynthesized color changes its neighbours' loads and the placement; bounded by once per run, the accept-only-if-not-worse rule of I3 and the user-driven next iteration.
  • Placement restart cost. The restarted first stage is a full stage; the seed from the previous positions should make it cheaper, to be measured in I7.
  • Cache identity. The mapped body changes while the pre-body and recipe stay the same; the io_env field must make the restore and the used bookkeeping unambiguous, and the LEC twin must be refreshed on every resynthesis.
  • Arrival-blind mapper. Until I8, the structural change comes from the budget ladder and the sizing tail, not from per-input arrivals; the gain may be smaller than a true timing-driven map.
