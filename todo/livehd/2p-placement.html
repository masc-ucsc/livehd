2p-placement Whole-color resynthesis inside final placement

Implemented and off: --set synth.place.color_resynth=true turns it on. The mechanism works end to end on colors of up to 17,000 cells. On the designs measured so far it keeps nothing, because every new body came out smaller and slower than the one it would replace. The work left is to make it accept: where it is called, how a new body is judged, and what the first synthesis is asked for.

The flow is on the place and route page, the code notes in pass/place/README.md (section "Whole-color resynthesis inside final"). The cone step it complements is synth.place.resynth; the stage-boundary whole-color step is 2is-incsynth.

What was agreed (2026-11-26)

  • Timing is first known in the final phase, so that is where the flow can decide how hard to push each color. A color only moves toward faster logic within one run. How far it was pushed is kept in the work directory, so the next run starts from it and does not push again unless its timing changed.
  • A resynthesized color is placed again alone; the other colors keep their positions. The new body still goes through buffer trees and sizing.
  • Timing trigger. A body that never had timing feedback is called once it fails. A body that had feedback and missed its target is not called again, whatever the difference. A body that met its target is called only when a requirement has since moved to not met, by 5 to 10% of the period.
  • Area trigger. Cones replicate logic: faster to run, often better timing, more area, lower pin density. Their area matters only when the cells do not fit a fixed core. Then the colors with the most cone area are resynthesized, replacing their cones. A generated core grows instead.
  • Priority. Fitting and routing are hard requirements; timing is maximized within them. Order when the core does not fit: a smaller body with no timing loss, then drop the legalizer's padding, then a smaller body at any timing.
  • Caps. One call per cone. Per color and run: one timing call and one area call. No more cones on a color after its area call.
  • Mapping. ABC does the technology mapping. A color is tried with two flows and the better result is kept; a cone uses one.

What exists

  • resynthesize_colors in pass/place/resynthesize.cpp: the replaceable cells of one placement block are cut from the placed, flat netlist, mapped against the measured arrival, required, load and wire lengths, rebuilt from the Liberty functions, proved by SAT, spliced in, and placed through the ECO path.
  • Timing call in the driver after the first sizing and buffering, before the repair loop. Area call around finalize_placement, with Options::terminal_unfit_keeps_padding so that the legalizer reports the failure before it drops its padding.
  • Cone rule: a root is tried once and the cells an accepted cone left are not cut again.
  • Test //lhd/tests:lhd_color_resynth_smoke: both calls, the size limit, the policy freeze, the usage error.

Why nothing is kept

  • The first synthesis is not area only. synth.delay_frac=0.4 gives every region 40% of the clock period. Mapping against the real boundary times is then a relaxation, not a push.
  • The call is early. Before the repair loop, minimax was at −296 ps where the loop then met timing. The step spends its time on colors that the cheaper repair fixes.
  • The comparison is uneven. A new body is timed before its own sizing and buffering, against an old body that has had both.
  • Cones are tiny today. At most four cones of 32 cells on the single worst path, and often zero attempts because synth.place.eval_budget is spent first. Their area is negligible, so ordering the area call by cone area orders almost nothing.
  • The two flows are one. ABC with and without area recovery gave the same mapping in every run. USYN cannot take an already mapped, flat body.

What is left

  1. Call it after the repair loop. Only colors that still fail once sizing, buffering and the loop are done qualify. Measure how often that is on the HighTide designs before anything else.
  2. Judge the new body after its own repair. Give the block's cells and nets one sizing and buffering pass before the timing comparison. This needs a block filter in the candidate scan of repair_buffers (edits() in repair.cpp), which has none today.
  3. Make the mapping a push. Find out why ABC returns a slower body against tighter required times: its delay model against the placement timer on the same cut, the effect of the wire-load table, and the headroom (10% of the period today). One number to produce: ABC's own slack for the old and the new body.
  4. Decide the first synthesis budget with data. Area first lost timing that this step did not win back (minimax +75 to −295 ps). Sweep synth.delay_frac over 0.4, 0.6, 0.8 and 1.0 with the step off and on, once items 1 to 3 are in, and compare final WNS, TNS and cells.
  5. A real second flow, or one flow. Either lift the cut back to logic that USYN can restructure before ABC maps it, or drop the second recipe and its second placement and timing run.
  6. Cones large enough to matter. Give the cone step its own evaluation allowance, raise the four cone limit, and record the area each accepted cone removed and added per color. Only then does the area trigger have something to order by.
  7. The area call on a real design. It has only run on the test frame. Find or build a fixed-core HighTide frame that does not fit, and check the order of "drop padding" and "accept timing loss" against routed results.
  8. Carry the push across runs. The flat result is not written back to the color's body, so the next run starts from the measurement in abc_cache/io_timing.json and resynthesizes the definition at the start. Check that this reproduces what final accepted, per occurrence where a definition has several.
  9. Colors above the size limit. color_resynth.max_cells is 20,000. Larger colors are skipped; decide whether to split them or to raise the limit after measuring mapping and proof time.

When it can be on by default

On the HighTide ASAP7 and Sky130HD designs that fail setup with the option off, it keeps a new body on at least a third of them, final WNS and TNS are no worse on any design, the cell count grows by less than the growth budget, and the routed results of two designs confirm the gain. Until then it stays off.
