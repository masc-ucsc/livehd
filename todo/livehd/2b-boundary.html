2b-boundary Partition-boundary environment for pass.abc

A colored region is tech-mapped as its own module, so ABC's SCL sizing saw nothing beyond its ports: no load on an output, an ideal driver on an input, no buffering of an input's fanout, and the whole period for every region a path crosses. Fix it the way every hierarchical flow does (OpenROAD's hierarchically linked mode): annotate port loads and drivers, propagate arrival/required budgets across the hierarchy, buffer a crossing net as a whole net, and size the stitched netlist. Design and status: pass/abc/README.md, "Partition boundaries"; code: pass/abc/abc_boundary.{hpp,cpp}, packages/abc.patch (the SC_Bnd table the SCL timer honours).
