Comparison and performance scope

The placement comparisons share their design's mapped starting netlist, macro/IO frame and authoritative constraints. CTS and repair may make arm-specific changes, which remain audited. CNN uses 65 SRAMs, Eyeriss 1,040 and LiteEth 5. The LiteEth reference uses routability-only placement after a preserved timing-driven rebuffering crash; this qualification prevents a whole-flow dominance claim. Both LiteEth arms configured a 1% repair-buffer option, but that option did not act as an observed cumulative hard bound.

Timing values in the main table are final extracted results only. Pending entries are not zero and placement/GRT estimates are not substituted. A supported-frequency claim needs fresh extracted per-clock STA, consistent generated-clock relationships, IO budgets and timing exceptions, and explicit setup/hold reporting. CNN uses the shared ASAP7 RVT FF 0.77 V / 0 °C standard-cell corner with normalized SRAM models and fixed 10 ps IO budgets; this is not an all-corner frequency claim.

perf record and perf stat accompany the physical evaluations. Closed-stage wall times are shown with their scope; an in-progress job has only a timestamped observed lower bound. These jobs share a 32-CPU host with other evaluations/builds, so their wall times do not represent exclusive hardware. The LiteEth final-route numbers exclude upstream placement and research lineage: the separately retained selected-job ledgers total 5,818.296 s for LiveHD and 4,601.480 s for OpenROAD, excluding their shared 335.583 s cold input. They are not minimal production-flow runtimes. Performance improvements will be reported with any timing, geometry or routing changes.
