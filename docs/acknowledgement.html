Acknowledgements

LiveHD is built by the MASC group at UC Santa Cruz and by everyone who has contributed to it. It stands on many open-source tools and on decades of research. This page credits the people who committed to the repository, the tools we use or studied, and the papers behind parts of the implementation.

Missing someone or something? Please open a pull request against docs/acknowledgement.html. If a part of LiveHD follows a paper or comes close to one, add it here. When in doubt, cite it.

Tools

Tools whose code LiveHD links, vendors or adapts, or that it runs as part of its flows, come first. Tools we studied for ideas, without using their code, are under Inspiration.

Linked libraries

  • Yosys — Verilog/RTLIL reader (inou/yosys) and the lgcheck cross-checker. Builds with sv-elab, SymFPU, cxxopts, libffi, flex and bison.
  • slang — the native SystemVerilog front end (inou/slang). The driver code is derived from upstream. Builds with fmt, Boost.Regex and toml++.
  • ABC — logic synthesis, technology mapping and CEC (pass/abc, pass/lec).
  • cvc5 — in-process SMT solver for LEC, formal verification and satopt. Brings CaDiCaL, Kissat, CryptoMiniSat, MiniSat, LibPoly, SymFPU and GMP.
  • LLVM — native code generation and ORC JIT for lhd sim.
  • OpenTimer (masc-ucsc fork) with Taskflow — static timing analysis (pass/opentimer).
  • DREAMPlace (masc-ucsc fork) — global, macro and detailed placement and routability kernels. Brings FLUTE, LEMON and OpenMP.
  • HiGHS — the LP solver for density warping (pass/place/density_warp.cpp).
  • tree-sitter — runtime for the Pyrope formatter.
  • Abseil, emhash, mimalloc, RapidJSON, Perfetto, Mustache, zlib, zlib-ng and zstd — containers, allocation, JSON, tracing, templates and compression.
  • GoogleTest and Google Benchmark — tests and benchmarks.
  • Bazel and its rule sets (rules_cc, rules_python, rules_foreign_cc, bazel_skylib, …) — the build.
  • MASC libraries: HHDS (graph and tree containers), HLOP (wide integers), iassert and tree-sitter-pyrope / prpparse (the Pyrope parser).

Vendored or adapted code

  • VieCut — min-cut partitioning, vendored in third_party/viecut.
  • Si2/Cadence LEF/DEF parser 5.8 — vendored in third_party/misc, used by inou/lefdef.
  • OpenROAD — FastRoute L/Z pattern routing adapted in pass/place/fast_route_pattern.cpp; OpenDB row cutting and tap-cell rules adapted in pass/place/handoff_rows.cpp.
  • wyhash and waterhash/woothash — hashing in core/.
  • Dmitry Vyukov's bounded queue — basis of core/spsc.hpp.
  • SLEEF — bit-exact expf ported for URoute inference.
  • dye — colors for Graphviz output; Wojciech Muła's linux-perf-events — perf counters; sfc64 from PractRand — core/lrand.hpp.
  • SkyWater SKY130 cells, OpenROAD-flow-scripts platform files and the TAU 2015 timing-contest fixtures — hermetic test data.
  • Contributor Covenant — the code of conduct.

External tools in our flows

  • Icarus Verilog and Verilator — reference simulators for checks, fuzzing and speed baselines.
  • yosys-smtbmc with Z3 — bounded equivalence of generated Verilog.
  • OpenROAD and OpenROAD-flow-scripts — DRC on routed DEF, the routing handoff, power-grid checks and the reference flow.
  • OpenSTA — reference timing in the synthesis harness.
  • Lean 4 with Mathlib and Isabelle/HOL — formal semantics and translation validation.
  • clang++ / g++ and Ninja — compile the generated simulators.
  • sv2v and Bender — CVA6 preparation; Graphviz — viewing graphs; ffmpeg — placement replay videos.
  • PyTorch, NumPy and Matplotlib — URoute training and placement analysis.
  • MkDocs, GitHub Actions, Renovate, Codecov and Prometheus — documentation, CI and monitoring.

PDKs and benchmarks

  • ASAP7, Nangate45 and SKY130 HD — technologies for synthesis, placement and timing.
  • HighTide — physical-design benchmark suite.
  • XiangShan, CVA6, PicoRV32, Ibex, NVDLA, Vortex and CORE-ET fixme — designs for simulation, synthesis, LEC and formal runs.
  • EPFL combinational suite, ISCAS and OpenCores — logic synthesis evaluation.
  • CircuitNet and TimingPredict — datasets for routability and timing models.

Used in the past

  • Boolector, pono and smt-switch — earlier LEC solver layers, replaced by cvc5.
  • yosys-slang — replaced by sv-elab; Google XLS — early interoperability.

  • replxx, spdlog, nlohmann/json, range-v3, sparsehash, parallel-hashmap, pybind11, protobuf, BitMagic, roaring, Boost and bazel_rules_hdl — utilities in earlier versions.

Inspiration

Tools we studied or compared against. Their code is not part of LiveHD.

  • OpenSTA — Elmore plus ramp-driver timing conventions for the native timer.
  • OpenROAD gpl/rsz/mpl/pdngen — density grids, slack weighting, repair order and macro thresholds in the native placer.
  • RePlAce and MLBuf-RePlAce — soft macros and virtual buffering.
  • Xplace — timing-driven placement weighting.
  • TritonRoute and CUGR — routing handoff conventions.
  • VPR — timing criticality exponent; PathFinder-style negotiation.
  • KLayout — a proposed independent DRC check.
  • Verilator — semantics for overlapping controls and combinational loops, and the simulation speed target.
  • ESSENT, GSIM and RepCut — partitioned, activity-aware simulation.
  • CIRCT (arc, firtool, ImportVerilog) — clock-gate enable modeling and the slang importer structure.
  • FIRRTL and Chisel — CheckCombLoops summaries and generated-code idioms.
  • Cranelift — the acyclic e-graph and ISLE rules behind pass/aegraph.
  • Google XLS — keeping loops rolled through the flow.
  • mockturtle, LSOracle and AnySyn — synthesis design references for pass/usyn.
  • Synopsys VCS, Design Compiler, Formality, Cadence Conformal and AMD Vivado — language-compatibility and LEC conventions.
  • CompCert, Vericert, Kôika, Kami, Vera, Alive2 and seL4 graph-refine — trust boundaries for verified translation.
  • LLVM Developer Policy — model for the contribution policy.
