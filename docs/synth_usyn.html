Register-Rooted Unate Synthesis
for DOMINO and CMOS

Methodology of pass.usyn (synth.mapper=usyn) and how it differs from the LiveHD ABC flow of synthesis.html (cited below as [S §n])

1Goal and Organization

A DOMINO gate [1] evaluates a unate function through one series-parallel pull-down stack; a DOMINO latch additionally holds the value as the cycle's state. If every register's next-state function, or a small decomposition of it, fits such a gate, the logic between registers collapses into one or two dynamic stages. Earlier DOMINO synthesis makes a network unate by output-phase assignment and then maps it [2, 3]. pass.usyn instead starts at each register, searches for a decomposition of its whole next-state cone into at most two DOMINO stages, and optimizes the remaining static logic natively, without ABC's logic synthesis.

Physical DOMINO emission is not implemented yet (target=cmos is the only accepted target). The DOMINO selection is a logical model: it is frozen, then expanded back into static CMOS, cleaned, and technology-mapped. Today's output is therefore a CMOS netlist whose structure follows the register-rooted selection. Fig. 1 contrasts the two flows; Table I summarizes the differences.

2Regions and the Logical Network

Coloring, the private copy and the cut are those of the ABC flow [S §2, §3], with the flop-to-flop profile so that no register's next-state cone is split across regions. Each region is translated to a RAW Lnet [S §3.3] in logical_state mode: Liberty, QN encoding and ICG absorption are ignored, every register bit and pipeline stage becomes an Lnet latch, and asynchronous resets, dynamic clocks and initial values become protected control outputs. The Lnet is imported into an XAG, a structurally hashed AND/XOR graph with complemented edges, sized against the budget before any search.

Definition 1 (Endpoint). An endpoint is a bit of a positive-edge register in a known clock domain whose next-state function Fq is computed inside the region. Its cone is the XAG transitive fan-in of Fq up to region inputs and register outputs. Negative-edge and unknown-edge registers are not endpoints; their cones stay static.

3Register-Rooted Endpoint Selection

Definition 2 (Cells). A DominoLatch computes Fq and stores it as the endpoint's state; a Domino cell is a phase-1 stage that feeds a DominoLatch; a Static cell is ordinary CMOS. A DOMINO formula is legal when it reads at most 8 logical inputs (x and ¬x count once, both rails being free), has a series stack of at most 4 and at most 10 factored parallel branches. Both polarities may be read, so binate functions are legal without phase assignment.

Selection minimizes a transistor proxy, used consistently by every search and acceptance test:

cost = 2·#AND + 4·#XOR + #NOT (static)  +  Σcells ( |formula| + 5 [Domino] + 8 [DominoLatch] )(1)

where |formula| is the transistor count of the pull-down network and static logic and inverters shared between cells are counted once. Each region starts from an identity baseline: one DominoLatch per endpoint fed by its unchanged static cone, which is always legal. Every endpoint then searches, under its own work slice, for a cheaper realization (Algorithm 1), and keeps the baseline if the search fails or runs out of work.

Algorithm 1 SELECTENDPOINT(q) — tiers in order, each bounded
  1. incumbent ← identity cell; admit a window: the whole cone or a reconvergence-driven window of ≤ 16 inputs
  2. one cell: the whole window in one DominoLatch; unate ⇒ exact minimal cover, else a bounded signed cover, series-parallel factored
  3. decompose F(B, R) = H(G0(B) … Gk(B), R): new phase-1 divisors Gi as Domino cells
  4. boundaries: enumerate cut frontiers (≤ 32) with contraction and growth moves; existing-divisor two-cell covers; wide-boundary round
  5. two-phase general tier; residual removal with care completion over ≤ 16 phases; witness-guided local divisors
  6. validate the best candidate exactly; commit iff its total cost (1) is lower

Functional decomposition over a bound set follows Ashenhurst–Curtis [4]; the covers are verified against truth tables of up to 16 inputs, simulated 64 assignments at a time. After all endpoints, pair and joint reselection revisit endpoints that share logic: a gate reaching exactly two endpoints in one clock domain seeds trials that re-select both together (both new, either alone), followed by sweeps over interface choices, private fanout-free windows, joint divisors and joint recoding; a trial commits only on a strict total-cost gain.

4Residual Optimization

Logic not absorbed into cells — primary outputs, unselected next-state inputs and the static inputs of selected cells — is optimized natively in a fixed order of guarded trials: multiplexer-chain balancing (accepted only if critical depth drops, no output deepens and cost grows ≤ 25%), 4-cut rewriting against all 222 NPN classes with an area and a depth template each, in the manner of ABC's rewrite [5], and windowed resubstitution (8 inputs, 32 divisors, ≤ 2 new nodes), the counterpart of resub [6]. Rewrites may not raise cost (1); an equal-cost rewrite must lower depth. Optional exact sweeping and AND/XOR balancing are off by default. The region is replaced only if its total cost does not rise, after which one feedback round re-selects the endpoints whose static inputs changed. ABC's dc2, &dch, &fraig and &mfs [S Table II] have no usyn equivalent; no step uses a SAT or SMT solver.

5Freeze, CMOS Expansion and Cleanup

The selection is frozen into an endpoint netlist of Static, Domino and DominoLatch cells with their functions, formulas, rails, phases and source-state correspondence; identical phase-1 cells in one clock domain are shared, and the whole artifact is validated (functions, legality, shared cost, phase order, clock domains) or nothing is published. It is then expanded into behavioral CMOS: one native register per original bit and stage, each cell's formula as static logic, no phase-1 storage. Expansion is structural and checked exactly against every register's reference next state.

The expansion is cleaned on a private copy with the residual optimizer of Section 4 over all outputs and next states, now across the former cell boundaries. With multi_rep (on in the second trial, Section 7), windows of ≤ 8 inputs also receive alternative representations — positive and negative SOP, a reduced ordered BDD [7], a bounded disjoint-support decomposition and NPN4 templates — kept as structural choices [8] and extracted for area and for depth, in the spirit of mixed-representation synthesis [9, 10]. The result is exported as an Lnet and reconnected to packed ports and native memory, ICG, instance and loop boundaries.

6Literal-Network Extraction (experimental)

In a DOMINO pipeline a register's output is stable during the first half of the cycle, while DOMINO gates evaluate in the second. Logic controlled only by registered signals can therefore settle in the otherwise idle first half and choose the literals a DOMINO gate reads, as precomputation does for power [11]. This is a step between expansion and the final cleanup (pass.usyn.literal_extract=n, n ∈ {1, 2, 3}, default 0).

Definition 3 (Literal network). Let a selected cell compute F(c, r) with n controls c driven by register outputs or primary inputs. Shannon expansion gives F = ∨v [c = v] · Fv(r). A template T(s1…sm) realizes all cofactors if for every v there is a substitution λv mapping each slot to a literal of r, its complement or a constant with Fv = T ∘ λv. The literal network realizes each slot as a balanced tree of 2:1 multiplexers over c (depth n) selecting λv(sj).

The cell then implements T instead of F; any template costs at least maxv cost(Fv), which bounds the gain. For every cell with ≤ 8 inputs, every control subset of size n is tried; the template is a cofactor's own gate, and an exact bounded search with ternary-simulation pruning finds the substitutions. Every accepted rewrite is re-verified over all input assignments. In the DOMINO flow the DOMINO cells and their literal networks are kept and only the remaining logic is re-optimized; in the CMOS flow (implemented today) the rewritten next states are passed with everything else to cleanup and tmap. Only the cell function is rewritten (mode a, the default). Cofactoring upstream cones (mode b) is reserved for time-critical cones once placement feedback exists, and admitting previous-phase DOMINO outputs as controls is a place-and-route recovery option; both add DOMINO stages or area.

7Technology Mapping and Trials

The logical design is mapped by a registered provider (pass.usyn.tmap=abc|none). Its contract is mapping only: it may neither re-synthesize logic nor treat proposed DOMINO boundaries as barriers. The ABC provider re-partitions the logical design without flattening, colors high-fanout scalar cones (≤ 128 nodes, ≤ 6 sources, fan-out ≥ tmap_sharing_fanout = 16) as their own partitions so that mapping does not duplicate them, and runs the region driver of [S §3.4] with a hook that maps every region with the map-only flow of [S Table II]. Sizing, the budget ladder and the boundary environment are identical to the ABC flow [S §4.2–4.4]; the area candidate, ware trials and memory lowering are off.

Because the static cost (1) only approximates mapped area, tmap_trials=2 runs the whole design twice: the base options, and a choice-rich variant (light cleanup before selection plus multi_rep). Each trial gets half of the remaining budget, minus 1/16 reserved for publication, and the winner is the design with fewer mapped gates (weighted by instance count), then smaller area, then lower maximum region delay. There is no silent fallback to ABC's synthesis flows.

8Budgets, Reuse and Determinism

One deterministic work budget (work, 4×109) is split into a structural ledger (preparation, import, baseline, freeze, emission) and a search remainder passed from region to region; each tier, endpoint and trial takes a slice and returns what it leaves, so results do not depend on wall-clock or lane count. A region larger than max_nodes (8×106 XAG nodes) or exceeding the memory budget is refused with the option to raise; large cores (e.g. minion) need higher limits. Selections are cached per region under a key covering the RAW Lnet, source semantics, names and policies, but not credits or mapping options; a row is reused only when the current credits are at least the recorded floor, so a hit replays the same search. Mapped regions use the provider's cache, as in [S §3.5].

Status. No physical DOMINO cells or characterization; negative-edge registers are not endpoints; the XAG is append-only (no in-place replacement or MFFC accounting); one feedback round. In mapped gate count usyn is still behind the ABC flow on several control-heavy designs (arbiters, comparators); progress is tracked in pass/usyn/usyn2.md.
