LiveHD synthesis cost vs design size

Wall time and peak memory of lhd synthesis on ASAP7 across 38 designs spanning 1 to 1.4 M cells, every one run through the same path: compile verilog → pass color synth → pass abc, so the small blocks and the full cores are directly comparable.

The unit that matters is the colour, not the design. pass.abc hands ABC one colour at a time, so a 1.4 M-cell design is never a 1.4 M-cell ABC problem — minion's largest single colour is 161,748 cells and its median colour is 1. Cost is charted per colour first, and the design-level charts below say what they actually measure.

ABC time per colour — the unit ABC actually solves

lhdtrack block lhdsuite core

One point per ABC invocation (2205 of them). pass.abc maps each colour separately, so this — not the design total — is the problem size ABC is handed. minion's 1.4 M cells are the SUM over 1311 colours: its largest single colour is 161,748 gates and its median colour is 1 gate. Log-log Pearson r = 0.90.

Inside ABC vs. the rest of pass.abc

inside ABC (sum of every colour) everything else in pass.abc ● lhdtrack block   ◆ lhdsuite core

The two series cross. Up to ~50k cells the per-colour mapping dominates and the remainder is a flat ~0.6 s floor. On minion it inverts: 2,246 s inside ABC against 10,965 s — 83%, just over three hours — outside it. A sample profile of matched_filter attributes that remainder to Mapper::optimize_ware (53% of wall) and Partitioner::build_module (39%): every ware trial is followed by score_ware(outlib, top), which re-imports and re-stitches the whole design. minion runs 1,142 trials at ~9.6 s each, versus 26 ms each on matched_filter — trials grow with the design and so does the cost of one, so this term is quadratic. Scoring a trial incrementally, against only the region that changed, is where the three hours are.

Peak memory — per design, not per colour

Total (whole synth flow) ABC only (its own allocation) ● lhdtrack block   ◆ lhdsuite core

Log scale on both axes — memory spans 26 MB to 5.5 GB across the set. Total is the peak RSS of the whole process, taken as the max over the three steps. ABC only is color_peak_rss_kb — the largest single colour's growth above its own entry footprint, i.e. what ABC added on top of the LGraph and the Liberty that were already resident.

Why there is no per-colour memory chart. color_peak_rss_kb is growth over the footprint on entry, so it measures execution ORDER, not problem size: across 2205 colours it correlates with colour size at r = −0.48 and 42% report exactly 0. Inside minion, a 97,279-gate colour reports 2138 MB while a larger 161,748-gate colour reports 344 MB and a 38,767-gate one reports 0. Answering "how much memory does a colour of size N need" requires instrumenting each ABC invocation directly (RSS delta around the call with a high-water reset, or malloc-zone statistics) — the current counter cannot support it.
